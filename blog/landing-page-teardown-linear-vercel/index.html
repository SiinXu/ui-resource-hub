Linear 和 Vercel 的首页是软件行业被模仿最多的页面之一。照抄它们的渐变没抓到重点；值得学的是一组决策，仔细读页面就能看出来。下方截图于 2026 年 10 月 14 日截取，两个网站都经常改版。

值得偷的决策

  1. 首屏只有一句定位，字很大，配一行简短说明。首屏不放功能列表。
  2. 产品就是首图。用真实界面（或忠实的渲染）代替插画，访客一眼看到自己将要用什么。
  3. 颜色克制。大面积中性色，强调色只留给操作和重点。
  4. 承诺之后紧跟证据：首屏下方就是客户 Logo 或具体的产品能力。
  5. 每个区块只讲一件事，一个短标题加一张能证明它的图。

不要照抄的部分

  • 它们的品牌知名度：大家已经认识这个名字，极简首屏才行得通；新产品可能需要说得更直白。
  • 需要专门团队才能做出的重度动效和 3D 渲染；一张清晰的静态产品图胜过一个平庸的动画。

Linear's and Vercel's homepages are among the most imitated in software. Copying their gradients misses the point; what is worth copying is a set of decisions you can see by reading the pages carefully. Screenshots below were captured on 14 October 2026; both sites change often.

Decisions worth stealing

  1. One sentence of positioning in the hero, large, with a short supporting line. No feature list above the fold.
  2. The product is the hero image. Real UI (or a faithful render of it) instead of an illustration, so the visitor sees what they would use.
  3. Restraint in colour. Mostly neutral surfaces; accent colour reserved for action and emphasis.
  4. Proof straight after the promise: customer logos or concrete product capabilities right below the hero.
  5. Sections with one idea each, each with a short heading and a visual that demonstrates it.

What not to copy

  • Their brand recognition: a minimal hero works when people already know the name; a new product may need a more explicit line.
  • Heavy custom motion and 3D renders that took dedicated teams; a static, sharp product shot beats a mediocre animation.

Checklist for your page

Can a stranger say what you sell after five seconds? Is the first image your actual product? Is there exactly one primary CTA? Does every section prove one claim? If yes, the gradient is optional.
